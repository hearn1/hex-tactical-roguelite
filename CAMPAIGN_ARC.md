# Campaign Arc

> **This document contains intentionally placeholder content.** Story premises, boss identities, and faction details are early drafts to anchor implementation work. Final narrative writing, monster art, and encounter pools are out of scope until later milestones.

## Overview

The first campaign follows a party advancing through four ordered acts of escalating threats. Each act ends with a boss encounter before the party can press forward. The campaign culminates in a final confrontation in Act 4.

Side quests are a planned major pillar of this campaign. Each act should be designed with explicit hooks for optional branches, alternate encounter layouts, and route variants — even if those branches are not yet implemented.

---

## Act 1 — The Verdant Threat

**Theme:** Nature / Goblins

**Premise:**
A quiet frontier region has grown dangerous. Goblin warbands, emboldened by something stirring in the deep wilds, have pushed out of their usual haunts and begun raiding settlements and blocking trade roads. The party is hired to push back the threat and find its source. As they cut through goblin camps and overgrown ruins, they discover a primal power — something old and feral — that the goblins are serving or simply following. The act ends when that force is confronted and broken, opening the road forward.

**Placeholder Boss:** A goblin warchief empowered by wild magic, or a nature elemental the goblins have been feeding — a creature of bark, thorn, and fury that serves as the primal heart of the local threat.

**Side Quest Hooks:**
- Rescue a captured caravan merchant who knows the region's old paths (alternate route unlock).
- Investigate a collapsed druid shrine that predates the goblin incursion (lore thread into Act 3).

## Act 2 — The Iron Wall

**Theme:** Goblins / Ogres / Skeletons

**Premise:**
Beyond the frontier, the party finds a fortified pass controlled by a loose alliance of goblin veterans, ogre mercenaries, and restless undead held in check by a brutal warlord. The warlord doesn't care about the primal threat from Act 1 — they care about power, territory, and the steady stream of bones their necromancer lieutenant supplies. The party must breach the warlord's defenses and bring them down in a single decisive encounter before the undead numbers grow too large to fight through.

**Placeholder Boss:** A hulking ogre warlord with a necromancer lieutenant — the encounter centers on one dominant melee threat paired with a support enemy that resurrects fallen skeletons, demanding the party prioritize targets carefully.

**Side Quest Hooks:**
- A skeleton patrol carries the badge of a noble house thought destroyed years ago (seeds a mystery for Act 3).
- An ogre deserter offers passage through a hidden route in exchange for help escaping the warlord's reach.

## Act 3 — The Ashen Choir

**Theme:** Skeletons / Cultists

**Premise:**
The undead were not an accident. Beyond the pass lies a region haunted by a death cult that has been systematically raising the fallen as a tide of skeletons to serve a greater ritual. The cult operates in cells, each one a priority target — destroy them piecemeal and the ritual fractures; let any cell complete its work and the tide grows. The party must hunt down multiple cult commanders across a scarred landscape before converging on the cult's sanctum. The act ends with the sanctum breached and the ritual broken, but the cultists' true patron — something arcane and vast — is revealed to be watching.

**Placeholder Boss:** Three cult commanders who must be neutralized before the sanctum encounter, followed by a fanatic high priest — a multi-priority fight where leaving commanders alive during the final push causes ongoing complications.

**Side Quest Hooks:**
- A cell holds prisoners slated for sacrifice; rescuing them yields a party ally or critical information about Act 4.
- An ancient tomb the cultists have been using as a staging ground holds a relic that could weaken the Act 4 antagonist.

---

## Act 4 — The Ascending Dark

**Theme:** Cultists / Arcane / Draconid

**Premise:**
The patron the cultists served is an arcane power — an entity of draconic lineage and corrupted ambition that has been orchestrating the chaos across all three prior acts, using the warbands and the death cult as sacrificial pieces to prepare a ritual of ascension. The party reaches its sanctum: a citadel suspended between planar layers, guarded by draconid soldiers, arcane constructs, and the last loyal cultist vanguard. The final encounter is a confrontation with the patron itself — a being preparing to shed its physical form and become something beyond the material world.

**Placeholder Boss:** A draconid arcane lord — a single dominant spellcaster with a bodyguard of draconid elites. The encounter combines a high-priority support kill (the bodyguard) with a final boss that grows more dangerous as the fight continues, rewarding the party for fast, decisive action.

**Campaign Completion:** Defeating the arcane lord ends the campaign. The party's Renown is tallied and a run summary is shown.

**Side Quest Hooks:**
- Intercept a draconid courier carrying the lord's contingency plans (weakens the final encounter).
- Find and stabilize a planar anchor point to prevent the sanctum from collapsing mid-fight (alternate encounter layout variant).

## Expansion Notes

- Act count is data-driven. A fifth act, alternate act ordering, or a shortened 3-act run should require only a change to the campaign definition file — no engine logic changes.
- Each act is designed to support alternate layouts: different entry routes, optional nodes, and side quest branches that don't change the act's completion condition.
- The arc above uses original fantasy content inspired by 5E tone. No official D&D IP, branding, or proprietary monsters are used. See `SRD_LICENSE_NOTE.md` for licensing guardrails.
